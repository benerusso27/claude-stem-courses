About these pages

Conventions

  • Slide references like "CH 4 slide 13" point to your instructor's deck for that chapter. "Ex 4.4" is a textbook Example Problem; "Prob 8.34" is an end-of-chapter problem. Problem statements are paraphrased.
  • Notation follows Mott: η for dynamic viscosity, ν for kinematic viscosity, γ for specific weight, hA, hR, hL for heads, NR for the Reynolds number.
  • Property values come from Mott's appendices (water: Table A.1/A.2; liquids: Table B.1; air: Appendix E). Use your textbook's values on graded work if they differ.
  • Slide notes flag the few places where a slide's number or formula looks like a slip. Ask your instructor if one matters for an assignment.
  • Every page works on a phone and has a light/dark switch in the top bar. The pages work offline once downloaded, except that fonts fall back to your system fonts.

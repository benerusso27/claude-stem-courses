Transverse shear: τ = VQ/It, shear deformation and built-up beams

Sign convention (Beer 8e): V and M as in Fig. 5.6: on the left free body V acts downward and M counterclockwise; M > 0 sags the beam. On a cross-section τxy is positive when it acts in +y on a face whose outward normal is +x, so V = −∫τxy dA: a positive V gives τxy < 0, and this page reports the magnitude τ = VQ/(It). Deflection y is positive upward (Beer Ch. 9); the page reports the downward deflection δ = −y. Tension positive.

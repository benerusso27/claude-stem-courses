Sign convention (Beer 8e): as in Fig. 5.6, on the left free body the shear V acts downward and the bending moment M counterclockwise; M > 0 sags the beam (bottom in tension). Distributed load w > 0 acts downward, so dV/dx = −w and dM/dx = V. Normal stress σx = −My/I with y measured upward from the neutral axis; tension positive. Curvature κ = 1/ρ > 0 for sagging. Blue solid = closed-form (textbook) solution; vermillion dashed / markers = independent numerical solution; open circles = sample "measured" data.

**Rationale.** The sequence follows the syllabus and keeps its two fixed labs (tension in week 3, torsion on the Olsen machine in week 7). Each lab lands one to two weeks after its lecture and avoids midterm and recess weeks. Because the syllabus teaches stress transformation (week 14) after combined loading and deflection, Mohr's circle is Lab 8. Lab 6 stops at stress components, and Lab 8 resolves Lab 6's combined-load case into principal stresses. Measurements carry forward: E and ν from Labs 1–2 predict G in Lab 3; the beam rig and gage technique of Lab 4 return in Labs 5 and 7; Lab 6's eccentric compression sets up Lab 9, which reuses the Lab 2 bar stock, Lab 1's yield strength and stress–strain curve, and Lab 7's clamp-as-a-spring model. Section numbers are Beer 8e's (the syllabus lists deflection as Ch. 8; in Beer 8e it is Ch. 9).

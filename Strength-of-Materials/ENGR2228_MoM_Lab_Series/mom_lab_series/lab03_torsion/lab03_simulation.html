Torsion: elastic, elastoplastic and noncircular shafts

Sign convention (Beer 8e): torque and angle of twist follow the right-hand rule; an internal torque T is positive when its vector points out of the cut section (along the outward normal), and φ is positive in the same sense. Shear stress τ and shear strain γ on the cross-section are reported as magnitudes for a positive T.

Deflection of beams: EI y″ = M, superposition, redundant supports, and large deflection

Sign convention (Beer 8e): x from the left end; deflection y is positive upward and EI y″ = M (Eq. 9.4); M > 0 sags the beam; V and M as in Fig. 5.6 (on the left free body V acts downward and M counterclockwise). Loads P and w act downward; an applied couple C is positive counterclockwise. Reactions: forces positive upward, couples positive counterclockwise. The finite-element solver is the instructor's beam_deflection.cpp, which works with y downward internally; this page converts its results to Beer's convention. Blue solid = closed-form (App. F and superposition); vermillion dashed / markers = independent numerical solution; open circles = sample "measured" data.

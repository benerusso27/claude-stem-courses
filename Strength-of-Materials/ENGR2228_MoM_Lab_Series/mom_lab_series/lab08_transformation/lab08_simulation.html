Transformation of stress and strain: Mohr's circle, strain rosettes, and a tube under combined loads

Sign convention (Beer 8e): tension positive; τxy > 0 acts on the +x face in the +y direction (Fig. 7.2); θ is measured counterclockwise from x. Mohr's circle: a face whose shear stress turns the element clockwise is plotted above the σ axis (Fig. 7.15), so X = (σx, −τxy), and turning the element by θ moves the point by 2θ in the same sense. Strain: ε(θ) = εx cos²θ + εy sin²θ + γxy sin θ cos θ (Eq. 7.41); Mohr's circle for strain plots (ε, γ/2) by the same rule. On the tube, x runs along the tube toward the free end and y is 90° counterclockwise from x as seen from outside the tube; rosette gage a is along x, b at 45° and c at 90°. Blue solid = closed form; vermillion markers = independent numerical method; open circles = sample "measured" data.
